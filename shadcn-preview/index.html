FAQ

Privacy and access

Are individual sessions public?
No. The public page exposes aggregate scale only. Individual supply is available to authenticated members.
Where does credential redaction happen?
On the contributor machine. Credentials and detected secrets are removed before upload.
Do sellers review sessions before publishing?
Yes. Sellers inspect the exact selected sessions before publishing a candidate.
